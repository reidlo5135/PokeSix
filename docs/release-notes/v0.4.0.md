PokeSix 0.4.0 — imported Pokémon show their level and real stats, and the AppImage joins the app menu.

**Added**

- **Level and in-game stats from your save**: Pokémon brought in from a `.sav` now carry their level and the six stats the game shows on its summary screen. Each squad card shows the level next to the name, and the detail view draws those stats in blue over the species' base stats (red). The scale grows past 200 in steps of 50 when a stat needs the room. Pokémon added by hand look as before.
- **Linux: the AppImage adds itself to the application menu.** Run it once and PokeSix appears in the launcher with its icon, pointing at wherever the AppImage is. Move the file and run it again to update the entry. Delete the AppImage and the entry hides; the README lists the two leftover paths to remove.

**Changed**

- 5th-generation save reading has now been checked against a real Pokémon White save (Black 2 · White 2 still only against test saves).

No data rebuild: 0.3.x data carries over. Squads saved by 0.3.x open as they are; re-import a save to fill in levels and stats.
