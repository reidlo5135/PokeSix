PokeSix 0.6.0 — the app is rebuilt with React, TypeScript and Electron, and catches up with everything 0.5.1 could do.

**What's in it**

- **Pokédex.** Filters for search (name, number or ability), generation, regional dex, game, types, base-stat total and per-stat conditions. List or grid view. The detail pane shows stats with a nature picker, abilities, defensive and same-type-attack matchups, and the learnset tabs: level-up (with Heart Scale hints and pre-evolution moves), TMs with where to get them, egg moves and tutors with their cost.
- **Obtaining tab (new).** The evolution chain with conditions and where to find evolution items, plus wild encounters for the chosen version. Gift, starter, fossil, static and event Pokémon from generations 1–5 that PokéAPI does not list are now filled in.
- **Items.** Categories, presence in generations 1–9, and per-game sources.
- **Squad.** Six slots with moves, ability, nature and held item, live problem list, defensive heatmap, physical/special split and resource tally. Undo/redo, HM slave, `.pks` import/export, and party import from Gen 4–5 saves (`.sav` / `.dsv`).
- **Town map.** Region maps with wild encounters, items and landmarks per location.
- **Settings.** Light / dark / system theme, generation rules, name and UI language (Korean, English, Japanese).

**First run** downloads the PokéAPI data (about 29 MB) and builds the local database once; this takes under a minute. After that the app works offline, except for sprites and map images, which are fetched as you browse.

**Not carried over from 0.5.1 yet:** the squad image export (clipboard / PNG) and the save editor screen. Squads from 0.5.1 are not moved automatically — export them as `.pks` in 0.5.1 and use Import in 0.6.0.
