PokeSix 0.5.0 — light, dark, or follow your system.

**Added**

- **Theme switch** in the app bar, just left of the generation button: ☀ light, ☾ dark, 🖥 follow the system setting. The whole window repaints at once, and PokeSix opens with the theme you picked last time. With 🖥, changing your desktop between light and dark switches PokeSix along with it.
- **Dark theme, first pass.** Backgrounds, panels, tables and text are dark, and coloured headers, type chips and buttons keep their colours. A few light-only colours have no dark value yet and look washed out on dark: the generation button, the "rules" pill, version chips, the type filter chips, the squad problem cards and the pale heatmap cells. They are next.

**Changed**

- Colours now come from one palette that can change while the app runs. The light theme looks exactly as in 0.4.1.

No data rebuild: 0.4.x data and squads carry over. A first start follows your system setting.
