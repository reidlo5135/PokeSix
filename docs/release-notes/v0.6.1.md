PokeSix 0.6.1 — faster screens and a squad screen that fits the window.

**Faster**

- **First visits no longer stall.** Opening the Pokédex, Squad or Town map for the first time took up to a second or more; it now takes about a tenth of a second. Pokémon icons are trimmed without re-encoding images, list data is cached per generation, and the other screens are prepared in the background once the data is ready.
- Long lists in the Pokédex and Items only lay out the rows on screen.

**Squad**

- The six slots fit the window height instead of scrolling. On shorter windows the type chips move into the card header.
- The card header buttons are back to the 0.5.1 set: change, clear and details.
- Pokémon icons sit directly on the header colour.

**Home**

- The bottom row reaches the bottom of the window, and Recent squads lists every squad with its own scroll.
- Problem counts on Recent squads show even before the Squad screen has been opened.

**Other**

- The window opens at up to 1440 × 960, within the screen's work area.
- The Pokédex search field is no longer squashed.

No data rebuild: 0.6.0 data, squads and settings carry over.
